Notes on Optical Recognition

The lined paper preview rotates while the spinner sweeps top-to-bottom. The output canvas renders the extracted document — Markdown, LaTeX, or SVG depending on the magic-action chosen.

Sample structure

Headings render in --heading (pink), body text in --ink. Inline code uses --accent-ink over --bg-deep.

A second paragraph here demonstrates how the canvas absorbs the dominant share of viewport height — about 70 % once the controls strip and chrome are subtracted.

Why a chip strip

The horizontal export chip strip is more space-efficient than a vertical stack and gives a one-finger swipe to reach all nine actions. Combined with a single-row prompt dock, the controls fit in roughly 130 px of the viewport.
